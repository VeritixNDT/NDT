FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which NDT methods are supported?
UT, MT, PT, RT, VT and ET out of the box, each with its own method-aware report template and acceptance-criteria fields. Templates are fully editable to match your procedures and standards.
Does it work offline in the field?
Yes. Veritix runs in the browser and keeps working with no connection. Your work syncs automatically when you're back online, with gap-free report numbering so two devices never clash.
How are reports kept audit-ready?
Each report records the inspector's certification, the calibrated equipment used and the acceptance criteria applied. On approval it's sealed into an immutable PDF with an approval stamp and a verification QR code. Work using unregistered gear or a lapsed cert is automatically held for senior review.
Can my customers see their reports?
Yes — issue a secure magic link and the customer gets a read-only portal of their approved reports. No account to create, nothing to install.
What does it run on?
Any modern browser — desktop, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install; sign in at veritix.io and your data is there on every device.
How do I get started?
Start a free trial at veritix.io — no card required. Add your company details, turn on the methods you run, and create your first report in minutes. For multi-site or enterprise needs, get in touch and we'll tailor a rollout.
